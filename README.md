# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Telecom Customer Churn Analysis is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn behavior, identify the key factors influencing customer retention, and understand the impact of customer churn on business revenue.

The project uses interactive dashboards to transform raw customer data into meaningful insights that can help businesses improve customer satisfaction, reduce churn, and develop effective retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecom companies because losing existing customers can directly impact revenue and business growth.

The business needs to understand:

- Which customers are more likely to leave the company?
- Which contract types have higher churn rates?
- How do payment methods and billing preferences influence churn?
- Which services are associated with higher customer churn?
- What customer segments should be prioritized for retention?

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and behavior.
- Identify key factors contributing to customer churn.
- Understand the relationship between contracts, billing and churn.
- Evaluate service subscriptions and their impact on customer retention.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Analyze the revenue impact of customer churn.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and business metrics.
- **Data Analysis** – Customer segmentation and churn analysis.


## 📊 Dashboard Overview

This project consists of four interactive dashboard pages.

### 1. Customer Demographic Analysis

Analyzes customer characteristics and demographic patterns to understand the relationship between customer profiles and churn.

**Key Areas:**
- Customer distribution by gender.
- Senior citizen analysis.
- Customer tenure analysis.
- Monthly charges across customer segments.
- Gender and contract-based filtering.

### 2. Service Subscription Analysis

Examines how different telecom services and subscription combinations influence customer churn.

**Key Areas:**
- Phone and internet service analysis.
- DSL, Fiber Optic and No Internet customer churn.
- Additional services among churned customers.
- Service combinations and churn patterns.
- Churn rate by internet service.

### 3. Contract & Billing Insights

Focuses on customer contracts, payment preferences and billing patterns to identify factors associated with customer retention.

**Key Areas:**
- Churn analysis by contract type.
- Payment method comparison.
- Average total charges by contract.
- Monthly charges by payment method.
- Paperless billing and churn analysis.
- Customer tenure across contract types.

### 4. Churn Prediction & Key Drivers

Explores customer churn indicators and identifies customer segments that may require retention strategies.

**Key Areas:**
- Overall customer churn rate.
- Key factors influencing churn.
- High-risk customer segments.
- Revenue impact of customer churn.
- Risk score distribution.
- Customer segment analysis.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns such as:

- Customers with shorter tenure may have a higher likelihood of churning.
- Month-to-month contracts can be associated with higher churn compared with longer-term contracts.
- Certain payment methods may show higher churn levels.
- Internet service type and additional service subscriptions can influence customer retention patterns.
- Identifying high-risk customer segments can help businesses prioritize retention efforts.

*Note: These are analytical patterns to investigate; actual findings should be validated against the dashboard results.*

 📈 Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Customer Retention:** Introduce targeted retention programs for customers with a higher likelihood of leaving.
2. **Promote Long-Term Contracts:** Offer attractive benefits and discounts to encourage customers to choose longer-term contracts.
3. **Enhance Customer Experience:** Monitor service quality and address customer complaints effectively.
4. **Personalized Offers:** Provide customized plans based on customer usage and subscription preferences.
5. **Early Churn Identification:** Monitor high-risk customer segments and take preventive action.
6. **Revenue Protection:** Prioritize retention strategies for customer segments contributing significant revenue.
